Stage Direction Essentials

Understanding theater grid terminology from the actor's perspective

Why is "Stage Right" on the Left side of the screen?

In theater, stage directions are always given from the actor's perspective while standing on stage facing the audience! Therefore, when you look at the stage from the audience/camera view:

  • Stage Right (SR) = Screen Left
  • Stage Left (SL) = Screen Right

Upstage (US) The back part of the stage, farthest away from the audience. (Historically named because older stages sloped downwards towards the audience!).
Downstage (DS) The front part of the stage, closest to the audience and footlights.
